## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an enterprise-grade, authoritative, and frictionless digital backbone for commercial and high-density residential property management across South Asia, tailored specifically for the operational demands of Bangladesh. 

The aesthetic is **Corporate Modern with Tactile Clarity**—prioritizing absolute data legibility, structural predictability, and transactional transparency. The visual tone balances the institutional solidity of property governance with the dynamic responsiveness of next-generation proptech.

### Brand Attributes & Tone
- **Authoritative & Uncompromising:** Dense property ledgers, maintenance operations, and governance records require immediate institutional trust.
- **Financially Resilient:** High-contrast financial indicators communicate health, delinquency, and reconciliation statuses instantly without ambiguity.
- **Regionally Attuned:** Calibrated for operational realities of urban South Asia—supporting dense utility reconciliation (DESCO, DPDC, WASA, Titas Gas), localized tenancy agreements, dual English/Bengali numeric legibility, and integrated micro-status indicators.
- **Contextual Demarcation:** Distinct multi-tenant role compartmentalization allowing Committee Executives, Facility Managers, Unit Owners, and Tenants to share a unified visual environment while retaining strict functional clarity.

## Colors

The color system deploys high-contrast operational roles against clean slate neutrals. It separates primary interactive triggers from financial verification states and governance tiers.

### Functional Mapping
- **Primary Interactive (#2563EB - Vibrant Royal Blue):** Reserved for forward operational actions—primary CTAs, row-level selections, tab switches, and system navigation links.
- **Institutional Anchor (#0F172A - Deep Trust Navy):** The structural base for root sidebars, high-priority data headers, and dominant textual anchors. Communicates immutability.
- **Financial Positive (#059669 - Emerald Green):** Strictly allocated to healthy financial indicators, settled payments, collection surplus, and maintenance completions.
- **Financial Critical & Reversal (#E11D48 - Rose Red):** Used for overdue rent schedules, bounce notifications, service tickets marked critical, and ledger reversal entries.
- **System Caution (#D97706 - Amber):** Highlights pending approvals, utility consumption spikes, and grace-period collections.
- **Governance & Community (#7C3AED - Royal Purple):** Marks community notices, general meeting (AGM/EGM) agenda items, committee elections, and executive voting resolutions.

### Slate Neutral Spectrum
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** Low-strain, neutral background preventing display glare in high-volume bookkeeping sessions.
- **Component Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity container fill for data tables, metric tiles, and transactional cards.
- **Tonal Dividers (`#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`):** Sub-pixel and hairline borders that structure dense matrices without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural display and dashboard titles with **Inter** for dense transactional interfaces, ledger rows, and systemic metrics.

### Typographic Principles
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Injected at heading and metric levels to inject modern structural presence without feeling whimsical. Its geometry ensures high recognition even at low display resolutions.
- **Inter for Operational Data:** Chosen for its balanced x-height, extensive tabular numeral alternates (`tnum`), and distinct punctuation glyphs. Financial ledgers, bill breakdowns, and meter readouts must consistently use tabular lining figures to preserve vertical numerical alignment.
- **Currency & Regional Rendering:** The Bangladeshi Taka symbol (`৳` / U+09F3) must be rendered in line with numerical data without vertical baseline deviation. When presenting currency values, the `currency-*` tokens mandate proportional tabular numerals with an integrated 2px right-margin between `৳` and the integer sequence.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an exact **8pt spatial rhythm** (using 4pt half-steps for micro-components and table padding). 

### Layout Model
- **Desktop (12-Column Fluid Grid):** Max content width of 1440px with a persistent 260px administrative left rail. 24px (1.5rem) gutters and 32px (2rem) page margins. Heavy data grids switch to horizontal table scroll modes beyond 8 stacked attributes.
- **Tablet (8-Column Grid):** Collapsible sidebar rail into a 64px icon dock. 16px (1rem) gutters and 24px (1.5rem) margins.
- **Mobile (<768px, 4-Column Grid):** Canvas padding locks to 16px (1rem) edge margins. Fluid vertical stacks replace multi-column dashboards. Critical actions shift down to bottom-docked application bars and touch-friendly drawers.

### Spacing Usage Rules
- `space-2xs` (4px) & `space-xs` (8px): Icon-to-label gaps, badge internal padding, and compact table cell heights.
- `space-sm` (12px) & `space-md` (16px): Standard form element gap spacing, card interior paddings, and utility summary lists.
- `space-lg` (24px) & `space-xl` (32px): Distance between discrete visual modules, dashboard metric rows, and ledger section splits.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages **crisp borders paired with ambient diffused drop shadows**, ensuring surfaces feel cleanly isolated without the haziness of aggressive glassmorphism.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Background Canvas):** Solid `#F8FAFC`. Completely passive.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8FAFC`. Outlined with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and supported by subtle ambient elevation: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 2 (Popovers, Dropdowns, Role Pickers):** Elevated above normal card flow. Retains `1px solid #CBD5E1` with amplified ambient spread: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Mobile Bottom Sheets):** Sits above a neutral dark backdrop overlay (`#0F172A` at 60% opacity). Outlined at top/all edges with `#E2E8F0` and shadowed with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard. The shape language projects structural discipline: corners are curved enough to soften dense bureaucratic forms, but angular enough to preserve an enterprise layout.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Standard Controls & Text Fields:** 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Data Cards, Modals, & Panel Modules:** 16px (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`) corner radius for outer containment.
- **Status Pills & Role Badges:** 9999px (`full-pill`) radius to immediately distinguish categorical indicators from actionable buttons.
- **Mobile Bottom Sheets:** Top-left and top-right radii set to 24px (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`), with bottom edges square to the viewport boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px corner radius, height 40px (desktop) / 48px (mobile touch targets). Hover state: `#1D4ED8`. Focus state: ring of 2px `#2563EB` offset by 2px `#FFFFFF`.
- **Secondary (Destructive / Financial Collection):** Background `#059669` (Collect/Verify) or `#E11D48` (Reject/Overdue Notice), text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#334155`. Hover state: background `#F1F5F9`.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Role Badges (Compact full-pill shape, 11px font weight 600, uppercase letter-spacing 0.04em):**
  - **Admin:** Surface `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`.
  - **Owner:** Surface `#EFF6FF`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`, text `#1D4ED8`.
  - **Manager:** Surface `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#334155`.
  - **Tenant:** Surface `#F0FDF4`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`, text `#15803D`.
  - **Committee:** Surface `#FAF5FF`, border `1px solid #E9D5FF`, text `#7C3AED`.
- **Financial Status Pills:** Incorporate a 6px solid colored leading dot alongside micro-currency notation:
  - *Paid / Verified:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, dot `#059669`.
  - *Due / Grace Period:* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, dot `#D97706`.
  - *Overdue / Defaulted:* Background `#FFF1F2`, text `#9F1239`, dot `#E11D48`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Structure:** 40px height, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, internal padding 8px 12px, font size 14px.
- **Focus:** Border transitions to `#2563EB` with an ambient glow shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Currency Input Variation:** Fixed left prefix containing `৳` wrapped in `#F1F5F9` boundary, tabular numeric formatting enforced on input keystrokes with auto-separators (`৳ 1,25,000`).

### Cards & Data Panels
- Rendered on Tier 1 elevation (`#FFFFFF`, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, 16px radius).
- Card headers enforce a strict 16px bottom gap with a subtle internal separator divider when displaying complex multi-tenant ledger aggregations.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: 18x18px, 4px border radius. Unchecked: `1.5px solid #94A3B8`. Checked: `#2563EB` background with white check glyph.
- Radios: 18x18px full circle with a centered 8px solid disc when selected.

### Mobile Sheet & Navigation Bar Conventions
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Height 64px, background `#FFFFFF`, top border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Four primary destinations: *Dashboard, Units, Accounts, Notices*. Active icons use `#2563EB` with 2px bottom anchor dot.
- **Mobile Bottom Sheet:** Draggable anchor handle (36x4px, `#CBD5E1`, centered 8px from top), internal body scrolling, anchored primary CTA pinned above the mobile home indicator bar.
