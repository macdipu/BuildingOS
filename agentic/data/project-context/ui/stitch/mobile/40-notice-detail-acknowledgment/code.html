Dear Respected Residents of Green Valley Heights,

Please be advised that the annual periodic mechanical descaling, disinfection, and chemical chlorination of the rooftop overhead reservoir and basement concrete water reservoir is scheduled to take place as per Dhaka North City Corporation (DNCC) sanitary hygiene directives and Building Operating Guidelines. Licensed sanitation engineers from Bengal Aqua Purifiers Ltd. will carry out the deep cleaning protocols.

calendar_month Operation Interruption Schedule
Planned Date Friday, 21 March 2025
Shutdown Duration 10:00 AM – 04:00 PM (6 Hours)
Affected Supply Lines

Cold water direct line, kitchen taps, master bath overhead feeds. Note: Standby emergency supply line at the ground floor security guard post will remain active.

checklist Mandatory Resident Guidelines

1

Store reserve water: Please store sufficient drinking and domestic utility water in buckets and domestic containers before 09:30 AM.

2

Secure all valves: Keep all interior faucets and stopcocks strictly closed throughout the maintenance window to prevent high-pressure airlocks when booster pumps restart.

3

Post-restoration odor: Tap water may carry a mild chlorine residual odor for approximately 2 hours post-restoration. This is nontoxic and verifies effective sterilization.
